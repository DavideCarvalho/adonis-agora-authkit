Calls between apps, from config only. With `service: { name, clientId, clientSecret }` in `config/authkit_client.ts`, a plain `fetch` to another app carries a cached token for `urn:agora:app:<target>` (an undici interceptor composed onto the global dispatcher). The target comes from the service discovery published on `Symbol.for('@adonis-agora/discovery')` (telescope), or from the static `apps` map (name → origin), which overrides it. Exact origin only, never across a redirect to another origin, never over an `Authorization` the app set. `service.nodeHttp: true` covers `node:http`/`https` too: tokens are pre-warmed (static apps at boot, discovered apps through the slot's `subscribe`) and refreshed ahead of expiry, and a request to a known app with no token ready fails with `E_AGORA_APP_TOKEN_UNAVAILABLE` instead of leaving bare. A wrong `service`/`apps` now fails the boot. On the receiving app, `service: { name }` exposes the calling app as `ctx.agora.caller` / `getAgoraCaller(ctx)` (server middleware registered by the provider). `ctx.agora` is always an object, typed by `AgoraRequestContext` in `@adonisjs/core/http`, which every Agora lib extends with its own optional fields; and `authkitService({ capabilities?, apps? })` protects routes only other apps may call. User requests are untouched.
